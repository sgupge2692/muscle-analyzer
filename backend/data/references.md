## スクワット系筋群マッピングの根拠文献

1. Lee et al. (2022). Differences in the muscle activities of the quadriceps femoris 
   and hamstrings while performing various squat exercises.
   BMC Sports Science, Medicine and Rehabilitation, 14(1), 1-8.
   DOI: 10.1186/s13102-022-00404-6
   → 根拠：通常スクワットでのハムストリングス活性化が弱いことを確認

2. Frontini et al. (2021). The Activation of Gluteal, Thigh, and Lower Back Muscles 
   in Different Squat Variations Performed by Competitive Bodybuilders.
   PMC7831128
   → 根拠：スモースクワットでの内転筋・外側広筋の高活性化を確認

3. Sports Science Insider. What Muscles Does a Squat Work?
   → 根拠：脊柱起立筋の体幹安定機能、ジャンプスクワットの体幹動員を確認
